Your free online toolkit for studying and productivity

Study Pilot brings together free online calculators, PDF utilities, and AI-assisted study tools in one simple workspace. Whether you are preparing for an exam, organizing class documents, calculating grades, or turning reading material into revision resources, you can explore practical tools without creating an account. Designed for students and everyday users, Study Pilot helps reduce repetitive tasks so you can focus on learning and getting work done.

Student calculators for everyday academic tasks

Use the age calculator to work out a date-based age in years, months, and days, the percentage calculator to solve percentage problems and compare changes, or the GPA calculator to estimate a grade point average from course grades and credit hours. These calculators are useful for checking arithmetic, understanding results, and planning. Always confirm official grading or eligibility rules with the relevant institution.

PDF tools to manage documents

PDF utilities can help simplify common document tasks. Merge several PDF files into one document, compress a PDF to make it easier to share, convert images into PDF pages, or extract PDF text into an editable Word-compatible format. Review converted and compressed files before submitting them because layout, resolution, or text extraction may vary by document.

Study tools to help you revise smarter

Study Pilot also offers study-focused tools including PDF summarization, document question answering, quiz generation, notes generation, multiple-choice question generation, and essay outlining. Use these features to create a first-pass summary, identify important concepts, practice recall, organize notes, or plan an essay. AI-assisted results are starting points: verify facts, answer keys, quotations, and interpretations using your original course materials.

Simple, accessible, and useful across your workflow

Explore the tools that match your task, follow the instructions on each page, and save your own work where appropriate. A calculator can check a numerical result, while a document tool can help organize information—but your judgment remains important. Keep backups of important files and avoid submitting sensitive information to online tools unless you understand how it is handled.
